About

A developer who ships.

I turn ideas into working software — in the open, on GitHub, and fast.

I’m Riad Developer, a full stack developer. I build web apps, Android apps, bots, APIs and MIT App Inventor extensions — and I publish what I build in the open on GitHub.

I work across the whole stack: TypeScript and React on the front end, Python and Node.js on the back end, Flutter and Kotlin on Android. And deep in the MIT App Inventor ecosystem, where I build extensions (.aix) and the Aixfy toolchain that helps other developers build them too.

My style is simple: ship early, ship often. PyRunner — my Android app for running Python projects on the phone — went from idea to 11 releases in a single day. With 128 repositories and 14 programming languages behind me, I don’t just learn tools — I build with them.
